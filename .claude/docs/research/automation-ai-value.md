# Why companies automate and adopt AI: citable statistics

Researched 2026-11-10 for anthonymuniz.dev (Services section). Every number below was traced to the publisher's own page, PDF or press release. Vendor-commissioned studies are labelled. Gartner items are predictions unless marked as a survey, so the page should say "Gartner predicts", not "Gartner found".

**How each quote was checked**

- **Read directly**: I opened the publisher's page or PDF and read the sentence myself.
- **Search index**: the publisher's site blocked automated reads (gartner.com returns HTTP 403; mckinsey.com timed out on every attempt), so the quote was matched through a search engine's copy of that exact publisher page. The headline of a Gartner press release is its exact title. Open these in a browser once before publishing.

## The statistics

### Adoption is wide, value at scale is rare

**1. 88% of organizations use AI, but nearly two-thirds have not started scaling it**
- Quote: "88 percent report regular AI use in at least one business function, compared with 78 percent a year ago." / "nearly two-thirds of respondents say their organizations have not yet begun scaling AI across the enterprise."
- Source: McKinsey, *The state of AI in 2025: Agents, innovation, and transformation*, November 2025. https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/mckinsey/business%20functions/quantumblack/our%20insights/the%20state%20of%20ai/november%202025/the-state-of-ai-in-2025.pdf
- Sample: 1,993 participants in 105 nations, all company sizes and levels, June 25 to July 29, 2025.
- Trust: **high**. The 88% vs 78% is confirmed by reading the Stanford AI Index 2026 chapter that reproduces it ("up to 88% in 2025 from 78% in 2024", source line "McKinsey & Company Survey, 2025"; https://hai.stanford.edu/assets/files/ai_index_report_2026_chapter_4_economy.pdf). The two-thirds sentence is search index.

**2. Only 37% see any EBIT impact from AI**
- Quote: "Thirty-seven percent of respondents attribute at least some EBIT impact to AI use, about the same share as last year."
- Source: McKinsey, *The state of AI in 2026: On the road to ROI*, August 2026. https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/mckinsey/business%20functions/quantumblack/our%20insights/the%20state%20of%20ai/the-state-of-ai-in-2026-on-the-road-to-roi.pdf
- Sample: 1,719 participants in 97 nations, May 4 to June 8, 2026; 36% work at organizations with more than $1 billion in annual revenue; weighted by each nation's share of global GDP. The 2025 edition had 39%.
- Trust: **high** (McKinsey's flagship annual survey, large n). Quote via search index.

**3. Only 5% of large firms get AI value at scale; 60% get almost nothing**
- Quote: "Only 5% of companies in our 2025 study of more than 1,250 firms worldwide are achieving AI value at scale" / "Fully 60% of companies are not achieving material value at all, reporting minimal revenue and cost gains despite substantial investment."
- Source: BCG, *The Widening AI Value Gap: Build for the Future 2025*, September 30, 2025 (PDF dated October 2025). https://media-publications.bcg.com/The-Widening-AI-Value-Gap-October-2025.pdf, press release https://www.bcg.com/press/30september2025-ai-leaders-outpace-laggards-revenue-growth-cost-savings
- Sample: 1,250 CxOs and senior executives who are AI decision makers, 68 countries in Asia, Europe and North America, nine industries. Revenue mix: 10% under $1B, 42% $1B to $9.9B, 23% $10B to $24.9B, 12% $25B to $49.9B, 13% over $50B. So 90% are companies with $1B+ revenue. Self-reported.
- Trust: **high**. Read directly in the PDF.

**4. Only 25% of AI initiatives delivered the expected ROI; 16% scaled enterprise-wide**
- Quote: "Surveyed CEOs report that only 25% of AI initiatives have delivered expected ROI over the last few years, and only 16% have scaled enterprise wide."
- Source: IBM Institute for Business Value, *2025 CEO Study*, press release "IBM Study: CEOs Double Down on AI While Navigating Enterprise Hurdles", May 6, 2025. https://newsroom.ibm.com/2025-05-06-ibm-study-ceos-double-down-on-ai-while-navigating-enterprise-hurdles
- Sample: 2,000 CEOs, 33 countries, 24 industries, February to April 2025, with Oxford Economics. Company size not stated in the release.
- Trust: **high**. Read directly. Note IBM sells AI services, but this is its research arm's CEO survey, not a product study.

### Pilots stall before production

**5. Only 25% of companies have moved 40% or more of their AI pilots into production**
- Quote: "only 25% of respondents have moved 40% or more of their AI pilots into production."
- Source: Deloitte, *The State of AI in the Enterprise* (2026 edition), press release January 21, 2026. https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/about/press-room/state-of-ai-report-2026.html
- Sample: 3,235 business and IT leaders (director to C-suite) across 24 countries and six industries, August to September 2025. Company size not stated.
- Trust: **high**. Read directly. Same release: "only 21% of those companies report having a mature model for agent governance."

**6. Two-thirds expect 30% or fewer of their GenAI experiments to scale, yet 74% say their most advanced initiative meets or beats ROI targets**
- Quote: "Over two-thirds of respondents said that 30% or fewer of their current experiments will be fully scaled in the next three to six months." / "nearly three-quarters (74%) say their most advanced initiative is meeting or exceeding their ROI expectations (43% meeting, 31% exceeding)."
- Source: Deloitte, *State of Generative AI in the Enterprise, Quarter four report: Now decides next*, January 2025. https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-shared/docs/about/2025/quarter-4.pdf
- Sample: 2,773 leaders, July to September 2024, 14 countries, heavy US and Europe: US 1,203, UK 200, Germany 150, France 130, Spain 100, Italy 75, Netherlands 50 (plus Canada, Australia, Brazil, India, Japan, Mexico, Singapore). All have AI in daily use.
- Trust: **high**. Read directly in the PDF. Useful pair: most experiments don't ship, but the ones that do pay off.

**7. 70% of the obstacles are people and process, only 10% the algorithm**
- Quote: "Seventy-four percent of companies have yet to show tangible value from their use of AI." / "with around 70% stemming from people- and process-related issues, 20% attributed to technology problems ... and only 10% involving AI algorithms."
- Source: BCG, *Where's the Value in AI?*, press release October 24, 2024. https://www.bcg.com/press/24october2024-ai-adoption-in-2024-74-of-companies-struggle-to-achieve-and-scale-value
- Sample: 1,000 CxOs and senior executives, more than 20 sectors, 59 countries in Asia, Europe and North America. Company size not stated in the release.
- Trust: **high**. Read directly. Older than item 3 (same publisher); use it for the 70/20/10 split.

**8. Gartner: over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027**
- Quote (headline): "Gartner Predicts Over 40% of Agentic AI Projects Will Be Canceled by End of 2027". Body: due to "escalating costs, unclear business value or inadequate risk controls."
- Source: Gartner press release, June 25, 2025. https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027
- Sample: a prediction, not a measurement. The release cites a January 2025 Gartner poll of 3,412 webinar attendees (19% significant agentic AI investment, 42% conservative). Same release: "33% of enterprise software applications will include agentic AI by 2028, up from less than 1% in 2024" and at least 15% of day-to-day work decisions made autonomously by 2028.
- Trust: **medium**. Gartner is the owner, headline exact, body via search index (gartner.com blocks automated reads). Prediction, and the poll sample is self-selected.

### Automation pays back fast when it ships

**9. Power Automate: 248% ROI, payback under 6 months (vendor-commissioned)**
- Figures: ROI 248%, NPV $39.85M, payback "<6 months" over three years, risk-adjusted. High-impact RPA use cases save about 200 hours per employee per year.
- Source: Forrester Consulting, *The Total Economic Impact™ Of Microsoft Power Automate*, July 2024, **commissioned by Microsoft**. https://tei.forrester.com/go/microsoft/powerautomatetei
- Sample: 7 interviewees at 6 companies (energy, professional services, financial services, entertainment in North America; pharma in Europe; global manufacturing), 7,000 to 100,000+ employees. Results modelled on a composite of 30,000 employees and $10B revenue, which also uses Power Apps.
- Trust: **medium**. Read directly. Vendor-paid, six interviews, composite model; Forrester says other companies' ROI will differ.

**10. Intelligent automation (RPA): 330% ROI, close to 500,000 hours saved in year 3 (vendor-commissioned)**
- Figures: ROI 330%, NPV $53.40M, payback "<6 months"; the composite "saves close to 500,000 hours in Year 3."
- Source: Forrester Consulting, *The Total Economic Impact™ Of SS&C Blue Prism Intelligent Automation*, April 2024, **commissioned by SS&C Blue Prism**. https://tei.forrester.com/go/sscblueprism/IntelligentAutomation/
- Sample: 5 interviewees (financial services, insurance, public sector; 400 to 42,000 employees) plus 166 survey respondents in North America, Europe and Asia Pacific. Composite: financial services, 10,000 employees, $4B revenue.
- Trust: **medium**. Read directly. Same caveats as item 9; a second vendor landing on the same "payback under 6 months" makes the range more credible.

### How much work is automatable

**11. Current technology could automate activities that take 60 to 70% of employees' time**
- Quote: "current generative AI and other technologies have the potential to automate work activities that absorb 60 to 70 percent of employees' time today." The same report sizes generative AI at $2.6 trillion to $4.4 trillion a year across 63 use cases.
- Source: McKinsey Global Institute, *The economic potential of generative AI: The next productivity frontier*, June 2023. https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/the-economic-potential-of-generative-ai-the-next-productivity-frontier
- Sample: modelling of work activities across occupations, not a survey. It is technical potential, not a forecast of what will be automated; McKinsey's earlier estimate was about half.
- Trust: **high** as a citation, but say "could" and "technical potential". Quote via search index (exact-phrase match on the McKinsey page). Three years old.

### Agents and customer support

**12. 87% of customers want a human option when a company uses GenAI for service**
- Quote (headline): "Gartner Survey Finds 87% of Customers Say Companies Using GenAI for Customer Service Must Provide Access to a Human Agent".
- Source: Gartner press release, August 4, 2026. https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-08-04-gartner-survey-finds-87-percent-of-customers-say-companies-using-genai-for-customer-service-must-provide-access-to-a-human-agent0
- Sample: 3,566 B2B and B2C customers, February to March 2026 (per the release, via search index).
- Trust: **high** for the headline (a survey, not a prediction; exact title). Sample details via search index.

## Not verified (do not use as is)

- **MIT NANDA, "95% of organizations get zero return" (*The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025*).** The publisher URL `https://nanda.media.mit.edu/ai_report_2025.pdf` now redirects (HTTP 302, checked 2026-11-10) to the NANDA group overview page; no copy is on an MIT domain, only on law-firm and blog mirrors. The report calls itself preliminary, and write-ups give two different samples (150 interviews / 350 employees / 300 deployments vs 52 interviews / 153 surveys / 300+ reviews). Use Deloitte (items 5 and 6) or IBM (item 4) for the same point.
- **Gartner, "at least 50% of generative AI projects were abandoned after proof of concept"** (article "Why Half of GenAI Projects Fail", https://www.gartner.com/en/articles/genai-project-failure). It appears on Gartner's domain in the search index, but I could not open it (403) or confirm its date or basis. If a browser check shows the date and wording, it would replace the 30% prediction as a stronger, backward-looking number.
- **Gartner, hyperautomation will "lower operational costs by 30%" by 2024.** Widely repeated; not found on gartner.com. Also a 2021-era prediction for a year that has passed.
- **Gartner survey of 822 business leaders (15.8% revenue increase, 15.2% cost savings, 22.6% productivity).** Attributed to the July 2024 release by news coverage only; I could not read the release body.
- **UiPath Forrester TEI.** The only UiPath-commissioned TEI found is from 2021 (97% ROI, hosted on UiPath's site). No 2024 or 2025 UiPath study on tei.forrester.com. Too old; items 9 and 10 cover RPA ROI.

## What best supports the pitch

"Most AI projects stall before production; I put automations and agents in production and measure them."

1. **The stall**: IBM (only 25% of AI initiatives hit expected ROI, 16% scaled), Deloitte 2026 (only 25% of companies moved 40%+ of pilots into production) and BCG 2025 (60% of mostly $1B+ firms report minimal gains). All are measured surveys of large companies, not predictions, which makes them stronger than the MIT 95% or Gartner forecasts.
2. **Why it stalls**: BCG's 70% people and process / 10% algorithm split says the gap is integration and operations work, which is exactly the service.
3. **The payoff once it ships**: Deloitte's 74% of most-advanced initiatives meeting or beating ROI, plus two vendor-commissioned Forrester TEIs both landing on payback under 6 months (label them as vendor-commissioned on the page).
4. **For the support-agent offer**: Gartner's 87% of customers who want a human option supports the bot's handoff to a person; cite Gartner's 40% agentic cancellation only as "Gartner predicts".
